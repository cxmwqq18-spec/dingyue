How-to · URL to Video

Turn a product link into a video ad.

Paste one product URL — AI imports the photos and details for you, then turns them into a ready-to-post video. No camera, no filming, no editing timeline.

Reading time · 6 minTool · URL to VideoLevel · First video

What you are building →

A vertical product video assembled from the photos on your product page, with a script AI writes from the listing details.

yourstore.com/products/canvas-tote
✓ Description parsed · ready to brief
Canvas tote bagreview the photos from the import3 found
Final result
“Show the tote carried on a morning errand — bright, natural, everyday.”

Five steps, start to finish.

The same job loop as chapter 01 of the workspace guide — the inputs are just different.

  1. Paste the product URL

    Copy the link to any live product page and paste it into the URL to Video field on Home.

  2. Review the imported photos

    AI reads the page and pulls the product photos and description. Keep the shots you want; the badge shows how many were found.

  3. Brief the video

    One or two sentences are enough. Estimated cost is always shown before you commit.

  4. Confirm and generate

    The confirmation modal restates the cost and your balance. Confirming holds the credits; a failed job returns them automatically.

  5. Collect from the Library

    The job moves through generating → finishing → completed. Download within 15 days — everything you keep is yours.

Good to know — Guests can build the draft without an account; it is restored the moment you sign in.

Questions people ask first.

Does the product page need anything special?

No. Any public product URL works — AI reads the photos and description that are already on the page.

What if the import picks a photo I don't want?

Deselect it before briefing. The video is built only from the photos you keep.

How much does one video cost?

The composer shows the estimated credits before anything runs. Nothing is charged until you confirm.

Keep going.

How-to · Image to Video

Bring one product photo to life.

Upload a single product photo and add a short prompt — watch the photo come alive as a moving product clip, ready for ads and social posts.

Reading time · 5 minTool · Image to VideoLevel · First video

From still to motion →

One photo in, one moving clip out. You describe the action; the model handles camera and motion.

Product photo → Generated motion
She picks it up and takes one swipe — naturally flushed lips

In this tutorial

  1. Pick one strong photo

    Clear product, plain background, good light — the same rules as the product-photo field guide.

  2. Describe one action

    Short and concrete beats long and vague. One motion per clip.

  3. Check the quote, confirm

    Cost is shown before you commit; held credits return if the job fails.

  4. Compare and keep

    Generate a variation if the motion feels off; download what you want to keep.

Good to know — One motion per clip. If you want two actions, generate two clips and keep the better take of each.

Questions people ask first.

What kind of photo works best?

One clear product, a plain background and even lighting. A phone photo is enough — motion comes from the prompt, not the source image.

How long can the clip be?

Pick 15 or 30 seconds when you brief the job. The composer shows the cost for each length before you confirm.

Can I use the clip in ads?

Yes. Download the finished clip from the Library and use it on your product page, in social posts and in ads.

Keep going.

How-to · AI Video

Make a video from product references.

Attach two or three product photos, describe the scene you want, and generate a campaign-ready clip — the model keeps your product consistent across every shot.

Reading time · 6 minTool · AI VideoLevel · First video

What you are building →

Reference photos in, one coherent video out. Same product, different angles, one message.

Reference 1 Reference 2 → Generated video
Morning routine with the tote — coffee, keys, out the door. Natural light.

In this tutorial

  1. Attach 2–3 references

    Different angles of the same product beat three near-identical shots.

  2. Describe one scene

    Where, who, what happens. The model fills camera work and pacing.

  3. Confirm the quote

    Cost is shown live; nothing runs until you confirm.

  4. Compare versions

    Generate a variation and keep the stronger take from the Library.

Good to know — References are just product photos you already have. Every version you generate is kept in the Library, so nothing you try is ever lost.

Questions people ask first.

How many references should I attach?

Two or three photos of the same product from different angles. The references anchor the product's look — more angles mean fewer surprises between shots.

Will my product stay consistent across shots?

Yes. Every shot is generated from your references, so shape, color and details carry through the whole video.

What if a scene needs another take?

Generate a variation from the same brief — both versions land in the Library and you keep the stronger one. Held credits return automatically if a job fails.

How-to · AI Image

Generate product images that sell.

Pick a tool preset, set the ratio and references, and collect store-ready product photos — main images, lifestyle shots and social crops from one session.

Reading time · 5 minTool · AI ImageLevel · First images

What you are building →

A set of consistent product photos: one clean main image plus the lifestyle shots your listing and ads need.

AI Video AgentAI VideoAI Image
Matte navy travel mug on a clean white background
Preset · White background1:1 ▾Generate
✓ 4 images ready · pick your keepers

In this tutorial

  1. Pick a tool preset

    White background for marketplaces, lifestyle for ads, social crops for stories.

  2. Set ratio and references

    1:1 for listings, 9:16 for stories; add one product photo to anchor the look.

  3. Watch the price confirm

    Each run quotes its credits up front — nothing is charged without a confirm.

  4. Collect the results

    Keep the winners, rerun the rest. Everything lands in the Library.

Good to know — Every run quotes its credits before it runs, and generated images stay in the Library until you delete them. Download the keepers.

Questions people ask first.

Can I get marketplace-ready white background images?

Yes — the white-background preset produces clean main images sized for listings. Pair it with the field guide on Amazon main image rules.

Do I need professional photos to start?

No. A few clear phone photos are enough as references. Better input photos make better output, but nobody needs a studio.

Can I make social crops of the same image?

Yes — rerun the same brief with a different ratio: 1:1 for listings, 9:16 for stories, 4:5 for feeds.

How-to · Product Links

Build your product library from links.

Paste product pages once, keep a clean library of every product you sell, and brief videos or images straight from a product card — no re-uploading photos.

Reading time · 5 minTool · Product LinksLevel · Setup

What you are building →

One card per product: photos, description and selling points, ready to brief from.

yourstore.com/products/canvas-tote
✓ Imported · saved to your library
Canvas tote bagdescription and photos parsed3 photos
  • Canvas tote bag3 photos · description parsed · ready to briefCreate video
  • Matte navy travel mug2 photos · description parsedCreate image

In this tutorial

  1. Import products

    Paste one URL per product; AI parses photos, description and selling points.

  2. Manage the library

    Rename, fix photos, archive what you no longer sell.

  3. Create from a card

    Brief a video or image straight from the product — photos attach themselves.

Good to know — One card per product is enough to start. Re-import the same URL any time to refresh the photos and description.

Questions people ask first.

Which product pages can I import from?

Any public product URL — marketplace listings, your own shop, landing pages. AI reads the photos and description that are already on the page.

Can I fix things after the import?

Yes. Every card is editable: rename the product, swap photos, rewrite the description. What you save is what future briefs use.

What happens when my page changes?

Import the URL again to refresh the card. Nothing in your library changes until you re-import.

How-to · Subtitle Studio

Subtitle a finished video in minutes.

Pick a finished video, choose the operation, and style the captions with a live preview — then burn in a new version, ready for silent autoplay feeds.

Reading time · 5 minTool · Subtitle StudioLevel · Polish

What you are building →

Most social feeds autoplay muted — subtitles carry the message when the sound is off.

Before
No captions
After
Bold 22pxWhite · shadowBottom third
Operation · Add subtitles ▾Style · Bold ▾Size · 22 ▾Generate new version

In this tutorial

  1. Pick a finished video

    Any completed job from your Library — the original stays untouched.

  2. Choose an operation

    Add subtitles, restyle existing ones, or translate to another language.

  3. Style it with live preview

    Font, size, position and color update as you click.

  4. Generate a new version

    A new Library entry; compare both and keep the one that reads best.

Will the original video change?

Can I translate the subtitles?

Yes. Pick the translation operation and target language; the new version keeps your video and swaps the captions.

Which style reads best on mobile?

Bold, medium-large text in the bottom third with a subtle shadow. The live preview shows exactly what viewers will see before you burn it in.

01How a generation job runs

Video and image tools all share the same job loop. Understand it once and every chapter after this one is just different inputs.

  1. Describe what you want

    Type a prompt, drop product photos, or paste a product link — depending on the tool. The composer shows an Optimize button that rewrites your draft into a structured shooting script.

    Generate now holds the credits and starts the job. A failed job returns them automatically.

02Home & the composer

Home is one composer with three tabs — Text to Video, AI Video, AI Image — plus quick-start chips and your recent videos.

  1. Pick the right tab

    Text to Video for an idea, AI Video for references, AI Image for stills.

  2. Write the prompt — or start from a chip

    Quick-start chips prefill a working prompt for common products.

Design spec · structured data

Schema 结构化数据设计。

每个 how-do 页输出 4 组 JSON-LD（服务端渲染进 <head>，随静态 HTML 输出，无 JS 参与）：

页面@type用途
how-do-*HowTo教程主体：name/description/totalTime/tool/step[]（每步 name + text，与页面 Steps 逐字对应）
FAQPage页面 FAQ 区块（q/a 与可见文案一致）
BreadcrumbListHome → Guides → How-to → {Tool}
Article作者/日期/封面，复用现有博客 article graph 形状
/guides hubCollectionPage + ItemList大卡片（workspace-guide）+ 7 张 how-to 卡 + field guides 卡，各为一个 hasPart ListItem
/guides/workspace-guideArticle（现有 graph 扩展）12 章长文沿用现有 page graph，补充 itemListElement 指向各章锚点
