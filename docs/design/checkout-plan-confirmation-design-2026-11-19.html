Before you pay

The questions we get at checkout.

What happens to credits I do not use?

Package credits are delivered monthly and expire at the end of each month of your term. Credits from a one-time credit pack stay valid for 24 months. When a job fails, its credits return to your balance on their own.

Can I move to a larger package later?

Switch to a bigger package from your account page. You pay only the difference for the rest of your term, and your unused credits carry over. Email support@aivideosugc.com if you need help.

Does the purchase renew on its own?

No. Every package and credit pack is one payment with no auto-renewal. When a term ends, you choose whether to buy again.
