## What is an AI Save the Date video?
An AI Save the Date video is a short wedding-date announcement built around a confirmed date and a small set of approved couple visuals. Its job is simple: help guests understand **who is getting married and which date they should reserve** before the formal invitation arrives.

The page should stay focused on **Save the Date video intent**. Couple photos are source material, not a competing photo-product target. Engagement storytelling can support the announcement, but the generic Save the Date query belongs here. Formal invitation wording, RSVP instructions, venue logistics, host information, and schedule details belong on the Wedding Invitation page.

## Save the Date video use cases

### 1. Wedding website announcement
Use a short landscape video near the top of a wedding website so guests see the couple and the confirmed date before reading travel or planning information.

### 2. Social media date reveal
Create a concise vertical or square edit for social feeds. Keep the confirmed date readable and away from interface zones, and avoid adding unverified venue or timing details just to fill the frame.

### 3. Destination wedding Save the Date
Pair recognizable couple visuals with a destination-style scene when the location is relevant. Treat scenic imagery as editorial context unless the real destination has been verified separately.

### 4. Family and friends announcement
Use a direct-to-camera or portrait-led version for messaging and email. The tone can feel personal while the central information remains the wedding date.

### 5. Save the Date video from photos
When the live product supports photo-to-video input, use a small set of consistent approved photos rather than a large mixed slideshow. Build short scenes that can be checked independently for identity, hands, jewelry, motion, and background continuity.

### 6. Relationship-story version
Add one or two familiar places or relationship moments before the date reveal. Keep the story short enough that it does not turn into an Engagement, Proposal, or full wedding-film page.

## How to make a Save the Date video

### Step 1 — Confirm the date before generation
The wedding date is the core fact of the page and the video. Verify it with the couple and the event plan before building the Save the Date version. If the date is not final, create a general couple or engagement announcement instead.

### Step 2 — Select approved couple images
Choose sharp, recognizable photos with compatible lighting and enough composition space for future crops. Use only images the people shown have approved for the intended publishing context.

### Step 3 — Choose one short story structure
Pick a clear sequence: couple portrait to date reveal, destination mood to date reveal, or relationship moment to date reveal. Avoid adding unrelated scenes simply because they look romantic.

### Step 4 — Generate or animate scenes separately
Create short, controllable visual beats rather than one long unreviewable sequence. Separate portrait, walk, destination, ring-detail, direct-to-camera, and closing scenes so each can be checked at full size.

### Step 5 — Add verified typography outside generated imagery
Do not rely on generated image text for names, dates, venue information, URLs, or invitation wording. Add real copy in an editable page or video layer after the visuals are approved.

### Step 6 — Review the destination crop
Plan landscape, vertical, and square versions only when the live product and publishing workflow support them. Recheck faces, hands, date text, and safe zones after every crop.

## Image, identity, and publishing quality standards
- Keep both people recognizable from scene to scene.
- Review faces, eyes, hairlines, teeth, hands, fingers, nails, rings, earrings, shoulder straps, and contact points at full resolution.
- Check that arms and clothing do not merge into the background or each other.
- Treat proposal and ring scenes as high-risk anatomy checks because hands and jewelry are visually important.
- Do not bake names, dates, venue text, buttons, SEO copy, or interface elements into formal source images.
- Do not present generated couples as real customers, testimonials, or proof of a live product workflow.
- Do not imply that a generated destination, venue, proposal, or event actually happened.
- Use editable typography for the final confirmed date and all factual details.

## FAQ

### What is an AI Save the Date video maker?
It is a video-oriented workflow for turning approved couple visuals, a confirmed wedding date, and a short story plan into a Save the Date announcement. The result should keep the date clear, preserve the couple's identity, and leave factual text editable for final verification.

### What should a Save the Date video include?
At minimum, include a recognizable couple scene and the confirmed wedding date. Optional supporting beats can include a favorite location, a short relationship moment, a ring detail, or a direct message from the couple. Formal RSVP and schedule details belong in the later invitation stage.

### Can I make a Save the Date video from photos?
Yes when the live product supports photo-to-video input and the people shown have approved the images for that use. Choose a small, visually consistent set and review each generated scene independently before publishing.

### Is a Save the Date video the same as a wedding invitation video?
No. A Save the Date mainly asks guests to reserve a confirmed date. A wedding invitation carries formal event details such as venue, hosts, schedule, RSVP instructions, and other guest-specific information.

### Can a proposal scene appear in a Save the Date video?
Yes as a short backstory beat, but it should not dominate the page or the final announcement. Users looking specifically for proposal storytelling should be routed to the Proposal page.

### How should I add the wedding date to AI-generated visuals?
Add the verified date in an editable text or video layer after the visual scene is approved. Do not rely on generated text inside an image for names, dates, URLs, or event details.

### Can I create a vertical Save the Date video for Instagram or TikTok?
Plan a 9:16 version when the live product and publishing workflow support it. Protect faces and date text from interface zones and review the final crop inside the destination platform before posting.

### What privacy checks should I make before using couple photos?
Use only approved photos, avoid unnecessary personal or location data, review the service's current storage and deletion practices, and do not upload sensitive images that are not needed for the announcement. Product-specific privacy claims must be supported by current first-party evidence.
