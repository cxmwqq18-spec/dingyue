What is it?

A date announcement first, a wedding invitation later.

An AI Save the Date video is a short wedding-date announcement built around a confirmed date and approved couple visuals. Its main job is to help guests understand who is getting married and which date they should reserve.

Engagement stories, proposal memories, rings, and locations can support the video, but the generic Save the Date query belongs on this page. Formal RSVP, hosts, schedule, and venue logistics belong on the Wedding Invitation page.

Quality standards

Keep people believable and factual details editable.

Identity — both people remain recognizable across scenes.
Faces — inspect eyes, hairlines, teeth, ears, and facial edges at full resolution.
Hands and jewelry — review fingers, nails, rings, earrings, shoulder straps, and contact points.
Typography — add verified names, date, location, and URL in editable layers.
Consent — use only images approved for the intended publishing context.
Context — do not imply a real proposal, venue, customer story, or endorsement that did not occur.

Pre-publish review

  1. Confirm the wedding date is final.
  2. Verify both names and every factual detail.
  3. Review each face, hand, ring, and clothing edge at full size.
  4. Replace generated text with editable typography.
  5. Check the final responsive crop.
  6. Confirm current product evidence and privacy wording.
  7. Keep indexation on HOLD until the Evidence Gate is cleared.

FAQ

Questions couples ask before creating a Save the Date video.

What is an AI Save the Date video maker?

It is a video-oriented workflow for turning approved couple visuals, a confirmed wedding date, and a short story plan into a Save the Date announcement. The date should stay clear and factual text should remain editable.

What should a Save the Date video include?

At minimum, include a recognizable couple scene and the confirmed wedding date. Optional beats can include a favorite location, short relationship moment, ring detail, or direct message from the couple.

Can I make a Save the Date video from photos?

Yes when the live product supports photo-to-video input and the people shown have approved the images for that use. Choose a small consistent set and review each generated scene before publishing.

Is a Save the Date video the same as a wedding invitation video?

No. A Save the Date mainly asks guests to reserve a confirmed date. A wedding invitation carries formal venue, schedule, RSVP, host, and guest-facing information.

Can a proposal scene appear in a Save the Date video?

Yes as a short backstory beat, but it should not dominate the page or final announcement. Proposal-specific search intent belongs on the Proposal page.

How should I add the wedding date to AI-generated visuals?

Add the verified date in an editable text or video layer after the visual is approved. Do not rely on generated text for names, dates, URLs, or event details.

Can I create a vertical Save the Date video for social media?

Plan a vertical version when the live workflow supports it. Protect faces and date text from interface zones and review the final crop in the destination context.

What privacy checks should I make before using couple photos?

Use approved photos, avoid unnecessary personal data, review current storage and deletion practices, and do not upload sensitive images that are not required. Product-specific privacy claims require current first-party evidence.
