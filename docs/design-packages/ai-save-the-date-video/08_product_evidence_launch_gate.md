# Product Evidence Launch Gate — Save the Date

## Indexation Status
**HOLD**

## Principle
Generated visuals and SEO copy are editorial assets. They do not prove that a live myDreamshoots workflow, speed, price, privacy promise, export option, or commercial right exists.

## Claims blocked without current first-party evidence
- instant
- one-click
- free
- unlimited
- no watermark
- 100% private
- commercial rights included

## Evidence required before indexation
1. **Input path** — current product screen or documented workflow showing how approved couple photos or prompts enter the Save the Date flow.
2. **Generation state** — current job/progress behavior; do not imply speed that is not demonstrated.
3. **Credits / pricing** — current first-party pricing or credit terms if cost is mentioned.
4. **Reviewed output** — at least one internally reviewed Save the Date result that matches the page intent.
5. **Photo privacy** — current storage, retention, deletion, and data-use policy.
6. **Consent language** — clear user responsibility for permission to use couple photos.
7. **Export formats** — verified supported output ratios or dimensions before claiming 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1 as product exports.
8. **Date and text editing** — verified workflow showing how names, dates, locations, and URLs are corrected in editable typography.

## Launch rule
If any critical evidence item remains unverified, keep `Indexation = HOLD` and remove or qualify the unsupported claim from the live page.
