### 介绍正文

**线上英文原文**

Our AI actors have lifelike eyes, natural hands, and a realistic voice, so the ad you download feels filmed with a creator.

You can start from a product link, a photo, or a prompt. A ready-made template keeps that shape familiar in the feed.

**原文中文翻译**

我们的 AI 演员具有逼真的眼睛、自然的手部表现和拟真的声音，让下载后的广告看起来像由创作者拍摄。

**拟改英文文案**

UGC stands for user-generated content. AI UGC adapts familiar creator formats, such as product introductions, demonstrations and unboxing videos, using AI presenters, generated visuals and scripted dialogue. For a business, it is a way to explain what a product does, who it is for and how it can be used through a short video.

Start with a product link, a photo or a written idea. In AIVideosUGC, choose a scene, prepare a product script and select an AI presenter where the format calls for one. Use your own product information to shape a video for a product launch, a social ad or a product-page introduction.

Explore the four formats below to choose how you want to introduce your product. Each format provides a different starting point for the story, from a direct product explanation to a demonstration or packaging reveal.

**拟改中文翻译**

UGC 是 User-Generated Content（用户生成内容）的缩写。AI UGC 借鉴创作者介绍、产品演示和开箱视频等常见形式，通过 AI 演员、生成画面和脚本对白呈现内容。商家可以用这类短视频说明产品的功能、适用人群及使用方式。

您可以从产品链接、照片或文字创意开始。在 AIVideosUGC 中选择场景、准备产品脚本，并在需要人物出镜的形式中选择 AI 演员。围绕实际产品信息组织内容，为新品发布、社交广告或产品详情页制作介绍视频。

下方四种内容形式可以帮助您确定产品的介绍方式。您可以选择直接讲解产品、演示使用过程，或通过包装揭晓产品，为视频故事找到合适的起点。
