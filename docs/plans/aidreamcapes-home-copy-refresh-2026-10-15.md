### AI UGC formats

- **Before (EN):** Give a First-Person Product Review — An AI actor gives a first-person review that sounds honest. The buyer hears a recommendation, not a slogan.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 撰写第一人称产品评测 — AI 演员会以第一人称视角给出听起来很真诚的评价。买家听到的是推荐，而不是口号。
- **After (EN):** Create a Creator-Style Product Review — Present key product details in a first-person, creator-style script. Check every claim for accuracy; AI-generated presenters are not actual customers.
- **After (ZH):** 制作创作者风格的产品评测 — 以第一人称脚本介绍产品信息。请核实每项宣传内容；AI 生成的出镜者并非真实顾客。

- **Before (EN):** Show the Problem, Then the Fix — The ad names a problem, then shows your product as the fix, said straight to the camera.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 先指出问题，再提出解决方案 — 广告中先指出一个问题，然后直接对着镜头展示你的产品，并声称它可以解决问题。
- **After (EN):** Show a Problem and Its Solution — Introduce a relevant customer need, then show how the product is designed to address it. Support each claim with verified product information.
- **After (ZH):** 展示需求与解决方案 — 先说明用户可能遇到的相关需求，再展示产品如何帮助解决。所有宣传内容都应以已核实的产品信息为依据。

- **Before (EN):** Walk Through How It Works — A short demo shows the product in use, one move at a time, so the buyer sees the action.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 工作原理详解 — 一段简短的演示视频一步一步地展示了产品的使用方法，以便买家能够看到实际操作。
- **After (EN):** Demonstrate How the Product Works — Show the product in use through a short sequence. Use clear steps to explain how it works and highlight visible features.
- **After (ZH):** 演示产品的使用方式 — 通过简短连贯的画面展示产品使用过程，并用清晰的步骤说明功能及可见特点。

- **Before (EN):** Open the Box on Camera — A first look as the product comes out of the box, before anyone reads a slogan.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 对着镜头打开盒子 — 在任何人阅读标语之前，产品从包装盒中取出后的第一眼印象。
- **After (EN):** Create an Unboxing Video — Show the package opening and the product as it is revealed. Check that the video reflects what customers receive.
- **After (ZH):** 制作开箱视频 — 展示打开包装及产品呈现过程。请确认视频内容与顾客实际收到的商品相符。

### Feature cards

#### 1. Product image

- **Before (EN):** Product Shots From Your Photo — A plain product photo can move in a clip. Match the color to the photo you uploaded before anyone posts it.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 用您的照片拍摄产品照片 — 普通的商品照片可以做成动态视频。在任何人发布之前，请确保颜色与您上传的照片一致。
- **After (EN):** Start with a Clear Product Photo — Upload a product photo as a visual reference. After generation, check the product's shape, label and color in the video.
- **After (ZH):** 从清晰的产品照片开始 — 上传产品照片作为生成画面的参考。生成后，请检查视频中的产品外形、标签和颜色。

#### 2. Script language and voice

- **Before (EN):** Write the Ad in Their Language — Write the sentence in the language you sell in, then pick a voice that matches it. No translation vendor has to sit in the middle.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 用他们的语言撰写广告 — 先用你销售产品的语言写下这句话，然后选择与之匹配的语气。翻译人员无需介入。
- **After (EN):** Choose the Script Language and Voice — Choose a language and voice that your setup supports. Listen to the narration, then adjust the script for your audience.
- **After (ZH):** 选择脚本语言和配音 — 选择当前设置支持的语言和配音。试听旁白后，可根据目标受众调整脚本。

#### 3. Actor delivery

- **Before (EN):** Direct the Actor's Delivery — If the script says the speaker is relieved, the delivery should sound relieved, not like a showroom tour.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 指导演员的表演 — 如果剧本中说演讲者感到如释重负，那么他的表达方式也应该听起来如释重负，而不是像在做产品展示一样。
- **After (EN):** Set the Actor's On-Camera Tone — Describe how the actor should deliver the message. Preview the video to check that the performance fits the script.
- **After (ZH):** 设定演员的镜头表达方式 — 说明演员应如何表达这段内容。预览视频，确认表现方式与脚本相符。

#### 4. Script edits

- **Before (EN):** Edit the Script Before Export — A price that changed this morning can be corrected in the editor before you export the file.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 导出前请编辑脚本 — 今天早上发生变化的价格可以在导出文件之前在编辑器中进行更正。
- **After (EN):** Keep Product Claims Up to Date — When product details or offers change, update the script before creating another version. Check each claim against your current, approved information.
- **After (ZH):** 及时更新产品宣传内容 — 产品信息或优惠内容发生变化时，请在制作新版本前更新脚本，并根据当前已确认的信息核实每项宣传内容。

#### 5. Captions

- **Before (EN):** Captions Sized for a Phone — Restyle the on-screen line so the claim is easy to read on a phone, even in a noisy room.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 适合手机阅读的字幕 — 重新设计屏幕上的文字，使即使在嘈杂的房间里，也能在手机上轻松阅读。
- **After (EN):** Review Captions Before Export — When captions are available, choose a style and check the wording and timing before export.
- **After (ZH):** 导出前检查字幕 — 视频支持字幕时，可选择字幕样式，并在导出前检查文字内容和时间。

#### 6. Closing frame

- **Before (EN):** Put Your Brand on the End Card — Add your logo, your colors, and your type so the last frame looks like the rest of the shop.
- **Before (screenshot translation):** 将您的品牌印在尾卡上 — 添加您的徽标、颜色和字体，使最后一帧看起来与店铺的其他部分保持一致。
- **After (EN):** Check the Video Before Sharing — Before downloading or sharing, review the closing seconds and check the product details, spoken claims and any visible brand elements.
- **After (ZH):** 分享前检查视频 — 下载或分享前，请检查视频结尾，并核对产品信息、口播内容及画面中出现的品牌元素。
