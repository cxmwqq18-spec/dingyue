HOW IT WORKS

From your product to a finished AI video

Add a product, cast an AI presenter and generate your video shot by shot — you review every step before download.

  1. 01Add your product

    Upload photo Import URL
    Example productDetails confirmed

    Upload a product photo or import a public product URL. Confirm the detected details before anything is generated.

  2. 02Choose a scene & presenter

    Product scene

    Pick a scene template and an AI presenter. Every presenter comes with a previewable voice and a ready-made setting.

  3. 03Shape your video brief

    ✦AI Video AgentYour video idea

    Describe your product, audience and creative idea. Choose the format and length, then check the credit estimate before you generate.

  4. 04Save & refine the storyboard

    ✓All changes saved↗

    Your draft is saved to the Library automatically. Review every shot, rewrite the lines and lock the takes you like.

  5. 05Generate & assemble shots

    Compare versions

    Generate shots one by one or all at once, compare versions, and your selected clips are assembled into one video.

  6. 06Review & download

    Review your final video Check every detail before downloading. Open my videos

    Watch the finished video with captions, download it, or export the script and individual shot clips for your ads and product pages.

Your draft is saved to the Library, and finished videos are kept for 15 days — download the ones you want to keep.
