HOW IT WORKS

From your product to a finished AI video

Add your product, choose an AI presenter and approve the storyboard — then download a finished product video, ready for ads and social media.

  1. 01Add your product

    Upload photo Import URL
    Example productDetails confirmed

    Start from one product photo or paste your product page URL. The workspace reads the photos and details for you — confirm them before anything is generated.

  2. 02Choose a scene & presenter

    Product scene

    Choose where the video takes place and who presents it. Pick an AI presenter, preview the voice, and the scene is set up around your product automatically.

  3. 03Shape your video brief

    ✦AI Video AgentYour video idea

    Write one short brief — what you sell, who it is for, and your idea. Pick a video format (9:16, 1:1 or 16:9) and length, then check the credit estimate before you generate.

  4. 04Save & refine the storyboard

    Shot 02 · Dialogue

    Hand-poured soy wax with up to 40 hours of burn time.

    ✓All changes saved↗

    The storyboard splits your idea into shots — product photo, presenter and dialogue for each one. Edit any line, save the draft to your Library, and approve it when it looks right.

  5. 05Wait for your video to generate

    ✓ Ready
    Generating
    Queued
    Generating your video — 1 of 3 shots ready
    You can close the page — follow the progress in your Library.↗

    Generate every shot and follow the progress here or in your Library. You can close the page while the clips render and come back when they are ready.

  6. 06Review & download

    Review your final video Check every detail before downloading. Open my videos

    Watch the finished video with captions, check the product and the voiceover, then download it — or export the script and single clips — ready for ads, social media and your product page.

Your draft is saved to the Library, and finished videos are kept for 15 days — download the ones you want to keep.
