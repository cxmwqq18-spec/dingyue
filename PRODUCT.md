# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Site operators, designers, and end customers use this platform to configure AI subscription storefronts, browse photo packages, launch AI tools, manage credits, upload assets, and receive private generated albums.

## Product Purpose

The product provides a multi-site AI subscription storefront and design system. Success means visitors can move from navigation to packages, tools, login, credits, generation, and album delivery without broken links or unclear routing, while operators can preserve changes through templates, generated pages, and publication snapshots.

## Brand Personality

Polished, task-focused, premium. The interface should feel capable and direct for operators, and cinematic but usable for DreamShootAI customers.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS decoration, over-rounded nested cards, tiny hard-to-click navigation links, hover-only experiences with no keyboard path, and duplicated template links that send different tools to indistinguishable destinations.

## Design Principles

Make navigation truthful: every visible link should describe the destination it actually opens.

Preserve template durability: front-end changes that affect generated storefronts must live in defaults, schema presets, runtime state, and rendering logic as needed.

Keep tools task-shaped: customers should land with enough route context to understand which tool or preset they chose.

Favor familiar controls: use standard links, focus states, and predictable route behavior over decorative custom interaction.

Protect multi-site boundaries: DreamShootAI-specific affordances should not silently expand the routing surface for unrelated sites.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default to keyboard-reachable navigation, visible focus indicators, readable contrast, stable click targets, and reduced reliance on hover-only interaction. Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA behavior where practical.
