FAQ

Questions about AI product photos

Can I use my own product photo?

Yes. Upload a clear photo of the product you want to show. Review the generated result before using it in a listing.

Will product details stay exactly the same?

AI can change small details. Compare logos, labels, colors, edges and proportions with your original photo before using the result.

How much does an image cost?

The image workspace shows the current credit quote for your selected settings before you generate. Visit Pricing to compare available plans.

Can I use the result in a marketplace listing?

Check the destination’s current image rules before uploading. Background, framing and permitted image content can vary between marketplaces.
