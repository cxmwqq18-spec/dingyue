A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Questions about AI Twin concepts.

Understand the preview and what you can do in the workspace today.

What is the AI Twin concept?

It is a way to explore a digital presenter direction for your product stories. Use the examples to consider the look, setting and message before planning your video.

⌄

Can I clone my face or voice here?

No. This page is a concept preview. Face cloning, voice cloning and an identity-matched digital twin are not available here.

⌄

Can I create a product video today?

Yes. Continue with available AI presenters and the editable workflow in the workspace. Prepare a brief, refine the storyboard, generate selected shots and assemble your chosen clips.

⌄

What should I prepare?

Bring clear product photos, the product facts you want to explain, your intended audience and one key message. These inputs help you shape a focused brief.

⌄

Are the portraits on this page my results?

No. They are illustrative examples of presenter styles and creative directions. Choose an available presenter and review your own generated content in the workspace.
