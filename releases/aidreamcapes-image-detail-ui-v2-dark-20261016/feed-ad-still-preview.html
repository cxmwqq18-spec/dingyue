FAQ

Social Media Post Answers

1. Is this a still image or a video?

This tool creates still images. For moving product creatives, explore our AI Video tools.

2. Can I use my own product photo?

Yes. Start with your own clear JPG, PNG or WebP image. Your photo guides the product’s appearance in the generated scene.

3. Can I add a headline or an offer?

Describe the space you need for a headline when preparing your image. Add final text, prices and promotional details in your editor after download.

4. How much does an image cost?

The workspace shows the current credit quote for your selected settings before you generate. Visit Pricing to compare available plans.

5. Can one product photo make several social posts?

Try different scenes or prompts using your own product photo. Check the quoted credit cost before generating each new version.

6. Where can I use a square social ad image?

You can prepare a square for social feeds or a product campaign. Check the destination’s current image and advertising requirements before publishing.
