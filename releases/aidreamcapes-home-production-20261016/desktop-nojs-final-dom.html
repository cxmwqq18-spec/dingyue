AI UGC explained

What Is AI UGC?

UGC stands for user-generated content. AI UGC adapts familiar creator formats, such as product introductions, demonstrations and unboxing videos, using AI presenters, generated visuals and scripted dialogue. For a business, it is a way to explain what a product does, who it is for and how it can be used through a short video.

Start with a product link, a photo or a written idea. In AIVideosUGC, choose a scene, prepare a product script and select an AI presenter where the format calls for one. Use your own product information to shape a video for a product launch, a social ad or a product-page introduction.

Explore the four formats below to choose how you want to introduce your product. Each format provides a different starting point for the story, from a direct product explanation to a demonstration or packaging reveal.

01AI Presenter Product Introductions

Introduce your product through a script spoken by an AI presenter. Focus on the main features, intended audience and everyday uses, so viewers can understand the offer before exploring the details.

02Problem and Solution Videos

Build a short story around a customer need. Explain the relevant product feature and illustrate how it could help in that situation, using product information you can support.

03Product Demonstration Videos

Plan a short sequence around a particular use, setup step or visible feature. A focused demonstration gives shoppers context and helps them understand how the product could fit into everyday use.

04Unboxing and Product Reveal Videos

Plan an opening sequence that reveals the product and highlights its packaging or visible details. Use your actual product information to shape an unboxing introduction for a new launch or social post.

FAQ

Questions About AI UGC Ads

How do I make a UGC ad with an AI actor?

Upload your product photo or import a public product page. Choose a scene and an available presenter, write your brief and save a draft. Generate and refine the storyboard, create the shots you choose, then assemble, review and download your video.

How much does it cost to make an AI UGC ad?

Choose a monthly or yearly plan based on the credits you expect to use. Each plan shows its price and included credits, and the cost of each photo or video appears before you start.

Can I use the finished ads for a client?

Yes. You can use the finished ads in client campaigns. Use product photos, voices, and other material you have permission to use.

Why are AI UGC ads effective?

AI UGC ads get attention because they look like the creator posts people already watch. Open with a clear problem, then show your product solving it.

Can I make a testimonial-style ad for TikTok?

Yes. Choose a vertical format and an AI actor to make a TikTok-style product ad. Check TikTok’s labeling rules before posting.

Can I turn a product link into a UGC ad?

Yes. Import a public product link, review its photos and product details, and use the confirmed information in an editable video draft. Generate the storyboard and selected shots, then assemble and review the video before downloading.

Can I make the same ad in another language?

Yes. Change the script and choose a matching voice to make an ad for another language. You can reuse the same product photo.

Can I make another version of an ad later?

Yes. Start from the same product photo, change the script or scene, and render a new version. You see the credit cost before each run.

What does AI UGC mean for a product ad?

AI UGC is a creator-style product video made with an AI actor and voice. You can make one from a product photo without filming or hiring a creator.

Can I change the ad after it is generated?

Yes. Change the script, actor, voice, or captions and make another version. You can test fresh openings without another shoot.

How long does an AI UGC ad take?

Timing depends on the selected model, video length, shot count and any revisions. You can save a draft, return from your Library and download the video after its shots are generated and the final render is complete.

Should I use an AI actor or hire a person?

Use AI actors when you need several ads quickly or want to reach buyers in different languages. Hire people when a real personal endorsement matters.

Will Meta accept an AI-generated product ad?

AI-generated product ads can run on Meta when they meet its ad policies. Check your claims and any required AI labels before posting.

Can an AI actor use a voice we already recorded?

Custom voice cloning is not currently available. Choose from the presenter voice options listed in the workspace, and review the supported languages before creating your video.

Do these AI UGC ads need a signed release?

No signed release is needed for our AI actors, and the download is cleared for commercial use without a watermark. Use only product photos and audio you have rights to.

Can I post creator-style ads without hiring anyone?

Yes. Use your product photo or confirmed product-page details with an available AI presenter. Refine the storyboard, generate the shots and review the assembled video before downloading.

How is a customer video different from AI UGC?

Customer videos are filmed by real people; AI UGC uses generated actors and voices. It gives your ads a creator-style look without organizing a shoot.

What should a small shop look for in an AI UGC tool?

Look for simple photo-to-video steps, realistic actors, and commercial downloads without a watermark. Check the credit cost before making each ad.
